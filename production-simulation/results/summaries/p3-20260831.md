## Verification and operational gates

- Every final job is committed with 560,000,000 rows, zero rejects, and no
  failed committed batch.
- Report and doctor outcomes pass. Bounded verification and optimizer reviews
  contain no failed or unknown checks; their documented incomplete coverage is
  closed by the full canonical digest.
- The Neo4j 5.26 recovery before/after source profiles have identical exact
  counts and schema. After excluding timestamps, paging metadata, and estimates
  derived from the corrected 1,000,000-row sample rather than the accepted
  999,810-row sample, both normalize to SHA-256
  `3ee124f60b0fa531763274def4e66ce35de9d6a5b38086d42fdfb10221153181`.
- Loader RSS remained below the 4 GiB gate. The final recovery load peaked at

  2.52 GiB.
- Loader and source swap/OOM counts remained zero. Loader disk use ended at
  42%, source disk use at 32%, and Flexible Server storage at 66.04%, below the
  80% stop threshold.
- The latest successfully posted Azure actual-cost observation was 437.39 USD.
  Later cost queries were rate-limited; the resources were stopped immediately
  after qualification, with substantial headroom below the 800 USD ceiling.
- Both controlled-fault evidence sets and all final result artifacts have
  verified SHA-256 manifests or recorded SHA-256 values. Retained guest
  evidence remains authoritative.

## Preserved limitations and deviations

- P3 validates one frozen migration profile, one deterministic large
  supply-chain topology, and the specified Azure target. It does not establish
  identical performance for arbitrary property distributions, Cypher
  transformations, network conditions, or target sizing.
- Recovery checkpoints prove safe continuation of the same immutable source
  and target job. They do not replace application-level cutover planning or a
  source write freeze for a real migration.
- Neo4j 5.26 required a 128 GiB source VM, 48 GiB heap, 28 GiB page cache, and
  5,000-row keyset-bounded discovery queries. Earlier failed attempts are
  retained as evidence and were not resumed.
- Azure governance stopped one earlier clean-digest attempt. Its retry used the
  same committed job and completed successfully; the deviation is retained.

## Resource state

All three P3 VMs were deallocated after final evidence collection. The
Flexible Server was manually stopped; Azure may automatically restart a
stopped Flexible Server after seven days. No resource, disk, database, Key
Vault secret, fixture, log, or evidence was deleted.
